How it works

From first email to a stocked machine

  1. We visit

    We come by, look at the space with you, and agree on the best spot and the products your building wants.

  2. We install

    We deliver and set up the machine. You provide a little floor space and a standard electrical outlet.

  3. We keep it stocked

    We refill the machine with snacks and drinks on a regular schedule. If you have a question, one email to us is enough.
